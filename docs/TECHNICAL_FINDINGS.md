## Encoding and build integrity

Text in the relocated story and ending tables uses game-specific atlas token indices, pointers and `FF` terminators; a raw CP932 sweep is not a text census. The HF20 CP932 scan yielded 1,905 raw candidates, largely tile-like binary patterns, and does not establish 1,905 untranslated lines. `source/audit_active_e000_hf20.py` enumerates five known active families totaling 241 structural entries, including pauses. The builder chain performs precondition and prior-build hash checks, writes only bounded resource regions, and recomputes the WonderSwan checksum; `source/validate_hf20.py` checks 157 changed HF19→HF20 bytes and permitted offsets. Build verification with a clean ROM was documented in the supplied audit, but could not be repeated in this packaging environment.

The original reports in `original-reports/` and the original source files retain the full per-version details. Reports for HF13–HF19 are historical and must be read with the HF20 RC gate decision rather than copied as global HF20 approval.
